First author · Geology · 2026

Detachment-involved deformation in the Santa Bárbara system

How do inherited rift structures shape present-day mountain building in NW Argentina? I combined a high-resolution earthquake catalog with receiver function images to investigate deformation beneath the Santa Bárbara system. We identified detachments at approximately 18 km and 8 km depth, with the shallower structure interpreted as a reactivated Salta rift detachment. The results show how the orientation of older faults influences where shortening is accommodated in the Andean foreland.

First author · Journal of Geophysical Research: Solid Earth · 2024

Seismicity and present-day crustal deformation in the Southern Puna Plateau

I used a deep-learning-based phase picker to build an improved regional earthquake catalog and investigate active faults and crustal deformation in the Southern Puna Plateau. The catalog reveals shallow strike-slip seismicity in the eastern plateau, contrasting with a broad seismically quiet western region, and a complex transition in the regional stress field. A short-lived earthquake swarm beneath Cerro Galán also points to possible fluid-related stress changes above its magmatic system.

Second author · Communications Earth & Environment · 2026

Transcrustal magma ascent beneath Laguna del Maule, Chile

This collaborative study connects deep magma transport with surface unrest at Laguna del Maule. We identified a zone of deep crustal seismicity linking magma reservoirs in the lower and upper crust. A pronounced seismic swarm in 2018 preceded faster surface uplift by about three months, suggesting that an injection of melt at depth occurred before shallow reservoir pressurization became apparent at the surface. The findings highlight the value of deep seismicity for understanding the development of volcanic unrest.
